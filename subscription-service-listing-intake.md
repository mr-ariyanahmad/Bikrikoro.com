# Subscription and Service Listing Intake

This checklist applies before publishing any future listing for a named subscription, premium service, cloud product, creative tool, media service, or account-adjacent service.

## Required seller information

| Item | Required evidence or wording |
|---|---|
| Exact offer | State precisely what is being sold, such as an official redeemable code, permitted gift voucher, seller-provided design service, or configuration service. Do not use vague terms such as “premium access.” |
| Fulfilment source | Provide the seller-owned stock source, reseller permission, supplier invoice, or other evidence that the seller may provide the offer. |
| Delivery method | Explain how delivery occurs without requesting or sending a customer password, OTP, recovery code, payment credential, or personal account login. |
| Buyer outcome | State the duration, region, limits, refund policy, warranty/replacement terms, and any eligibility requirements honestly. |
| Brand wording | Do not describe the product as “official,” “authorized,” or affiliated unless that status can be demonstrated. |

## Disallowed listing patterns

Shared subscriptions, borrowed access, transferred Gmail or cloud accounts, passwords, OTPs, recovery codes, bypass instructions, and access obtained through another person’s account must not be listed. A seller may instead offer a legitimate service completed in the buyer’s own account, provided the service scope is explicit and no credential is shared in chat or public listing content.

## Example safe listing directions

| Requested area | Possible compliant direction after evidence review |
|---|---|
| Netflix, YouTube, Spotify, Canva, CapCut | Officially permitted voucher, gift code, or authorized reseller offer with exact region, duration, delivery, and refund terms. |
| Google Cloud or Gmail | Buyer-owned account setup, migration, workspace configuration, training, or support service; never account resale or credential transfer. |
| Design and editing tools | Seller-created templates, legitimate license transfer where permitted, or scoped creative service completed for the buyer. |

> No branded subscription or service listing should be published until the seller confirms the exact stock or service scope and the lawful fulfilment route for that individual product.
